# Blog Surface

## Scope

Persian RTL static blog index (`blogs.html`), linked from the existing landing page. Visitor mode: Read. The article detail page (`blog.html`) was removed, so the index is the whole blog surface: titles are plain text and the category chips are the only navigation.

## Audience and Job

Organizations and project owners evaluating machinery and support for pulp-paper and fluff-pulp production lines. They need clear introductory guidance on production lines and feedstocks, and a path to consultation.

## Content and Constraints

Use several clearly identified sample entries, including one hypothetical educational article about getting started with a fluff-pulp line. Do not invent customers, capacities, performance results, article dates, or author identities. Existing Persian RTL, Vazirmatn, blue-gray surfaces, orange accent, logo, and soft raised/inset treatment are incumbent commitments. Keep normal document scrolling and readable article measure. Blog filters are in scope; search, CMS, pagination, and backend are not.

## Chosen Direction

The user selected a V2 extension of the homepage's existing neumorphic design system. Keep the Persian RTL identity and existing topic-guide composition, adding a searchable, four-card sample index and a more expressive hero without presenting invented editorial facts as real.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Help a project owner move from a production question to a relevant technical article. Refuse the generic wall of interchangeable cards.

OWN-WORLD: Inherit Pulp Iran's Persian RTL identity, local Vazirmatn, pale blue-gray ground, orange emphasis, dark slate text, soft raised and inset surfaces, and established logo.

STORY: Readers orient through the topic rail, search by title or topic, open clearly labeled hypothetical guides, follow their section headings, and can return to the index or request consultation. Sample counts describe this page only; no author identities are supplied.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A compact floating masthead sits above a two-column index. A soft-gradient neumorphic hero contains a large two-part title, short introduction, accessible live-search control, and a compact statbar for four sample articles, three categories, and zero identified authors. Below it, the raised featured article and raised topic rail share the viewport. The four sample cards continue below in a responsive grid. The article page keeps the same masthead and a calm, readable text column.

FORM: Retain the topic-guide form and inherit the homepage's V1 neumorphic tokens: paired raised/inset shadows, translucent white edges, Vazirmatn, the blue-gray ground, orange accents, and its animated CTA ring. Use the rail as compact wayfinding, not a second card grid; animate article cards only where native view timelines are supported, with visible-by-default fallback.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
